The Data Acquisition Unit is a USB lab instrument for university students learning the basics of host-to-device and device-to-host communication over USB. It is manufactured and sold by Bibeat Ltd., a non-share-holding company that promotes indigenously developed devices in collaboration with the Department of Biomedical Physics & Technology, University of Dhaka.

I upgraded the unit's desktop software, written in C#, which drives the device and displays the acquired data.
